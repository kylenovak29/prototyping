Why does the energy bar work?

These are orthonormal bases for the same Hilbert space, L²[0,1]. Every selected component is an orthogonal projection, and the residual is orthogonal to the reconstruction. Thus the squared norm splits exactly:

∫₀¹ |f|² dx = ∑selected n |cₙ|² + ∫₀¹ |f − fS|² dx

The amber shading shows where the curves differ. Unlike the shaded area, the amber portion of the bar represents integrated squared error, not the largest pointwise deviation. Switching from cosine to shifted Legendre changes the nine-dimensional approximation space, but not the inner product.
